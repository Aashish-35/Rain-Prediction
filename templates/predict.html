How it works

A Random Forest classifier, trained with SMOTE to handle the rare rainy days, estimates the probability of rain from five readings.

Humidity, cloud cover and temperature are the strongest signals in the training data.

Input ranges

Values are limited to the ranges the model was trained on so results stay reliable.

  • 🌡️ Temperature: 10 to 35 °C
  • 💧 Humidity: 30 to 100 %
  • 🌬️ Wind speed: 0 to 20 km/h
  • ☁️ Cloud cover: 0 to 100 %
  • 🧭 Pressure: 980 to 1050 hPa
